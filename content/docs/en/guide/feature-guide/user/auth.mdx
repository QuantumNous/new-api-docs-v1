## Log In with a Username and Password

**Steps**

1. Click “Log in” in the upper-right corner of the site to open the login page.
2. Enter your username or the email address linked to your account in “Username or email”.
3. Enter your password in “Password”.
4. If the page shows a human verification check, follow the prompts until it displays “Success!”.
5. If the page shows an agreement checkbox, select “I have read and agree to …”.
6. Click “Log in”.
7. If your account has two-step verification enabled or a registered Passkey, follow the steps in [Complete Login Verification](#complete-login-verification).

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- After a successful login, the site displays “Welcome back!” and opens the console. If you were redirected from a page that requires login, you return to that page.
- The login page has no “Remember me” option. The system keeps you logged in for a fixed period of 30 days from the time you log in. When that period ends, log in again.
- Complete human verification again each time you click “Log in”.
- If the administrator disables password login, the login page hides “Username or email”, “Password”, and “Forgot password?”. Use another login method.
</Callout>

## Log In with a Third-Party Account or Passkey

**Steps**

1. If the page shows an agreement checkbox, select “I have read and agree to …” first.
2. Click “Log in with Passkey” or one of the “Continue with …” buttons below “Or continue with”.
3. Complete verification or authorization as described in the table below.
4. If you used a third-party account and your account has two-step verification enabled or a registered Passkey, follow the steps in [Complete Login Verification](#complete-login-verification).

## Complete Login Verification

If your account has two-step verification enabled or a registered Passkey, logging in with a username and password, WeChat, or another third-party account opens the “Security Verification” page and displays the “Complete Login” dialog with the message “Please verify your identity to complete login.”

**Steps**

1. At the top of the dialog, select “Authenticator Code” or “Passkey”. Only methods enabled for your account are shown.
2. If you select “Authenticator Code”, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app or an unused backup code in “Authenticator code or backup code”.
3. Click “Verify”. If you selected “Passkey”, follow the browser or system prompts to confirm.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Complete verification within 5 minutes. If the time limit expires, the page displays “The login process has expired. Please log in again.” Refreshing the page returns you to the login page, where you must log in again.
- After 5 consecutive incorrect attempts, verification is locked for 5 minutes and the page displays “Two-step verification is temporarily locked.”
- Each backup code can be used only once. For instructions on enabling two-step verification and saving backup codes, see <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/security#enable-two-step-verification" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Security &amp; Access</a>.
</Callout>

## Create an Account

- Registration and Login – Registration

![Registration and Login – Registration page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/registration-and-login-register.png)

**Steps**

1. Click “Sign up” below the login page heading or “Get started” on the home page to open the “Create an account” page.
2. Fill in “Username”, “Password”, and “Confirm password”.
3. If the page shows “Email (verification required)”, enter your email address, click “Send verification code”, then enter the code from the email.
4. If the page shows a human verification check, follow the prompts to complete it.
5. If the page shows an agreement checkbox, select “I have read and agree to …”.
6. Click “Create account”.
7. When you are redirected to the login page, log in with the username and password you just registered.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Below “Password”, the page shows a strength rating (“Weak”, “Fair”, “Good”, or “Strong”) and suggestions. Under “Password requirements”, the allowed length is 8–128 characters. If the password is too short or too long, the page displays “… more characters needed” or “… characters over the limit”. Under “Make your password stronger”, the page lists “At least 15 characters”, “Includes both uppercase and lowercase letters”, “Includes a number”, and “Includes a symbol”. Each suggestion is checked when met. You can register without meeting these suggestions. If the entire password is a common password (optionally followed by numbers or symbols), a single repeated character, or a sequence of consecutive characters such as `12345678`, the page also displays “Easy to guess” and rates the password as “Weak”. You can still register, but choosing a different password is recommended.
- The email and verification code fields appear below “Confirm password” only if the administrator enables email verification. Email verification is disabled in the screenshot, so neither field appears.
- After successful registration, the page displays “Account created! Please log in.” Log in manually to continue. Accounts created through registration have the standard user role.
- The registration page has no invitation code field. If you open the registration page through someone’s invitation link and register, the site automatically records the referral. See <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/wallet#transfer-referral-rewards" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wallet</a>.
- You can also click a third-party account button below “Create account” to create an account on your first login. See [Log In with a Third-Party Account or Passkey](#log-in-with-a-third-party-account-or-passkey).
- If the site does not require email verification, registration leaves your account without a linked email address, so you cannot reset a forgotten password by email. After logging in, link an email address in <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/security#link-your-email-address-and-third-party-accounts" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Security &amp; Access</a>.
</Callout>

## Reset a Forgotten Password

- Registration and Login – Forgot Password

![Registration and Login – Forgot Password page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/registration-and-login-forgot-password-reset.png)

**Steps**

1. Click “Forgot password?” above and to the right of “Password” on the login page to open the “Forgot Password” page.
2. In “Email”, enter the email address linked to your account.
3. If the page shows a human verification check, follow the prompts to complete it.
4. Click “Send reset email”.
5. Open the email with the subject “… Password Reset” and click “here” in the email.
6. On the “Reset Password” page, verify the email address shown in “Email” and click “Confirm password reset”.
7. Click the copy button to the right of “New password” and save the password generated by the system.
8. Click “Back to login” and log in with the new password.
9. After logging in, set a password of your choice in <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/security#change-or-set-your-password" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Security &amp; Access</a>.

<Callout type="warn" title="Caution">

- The system generates a 12-character password made up of letters and numbers. You can set your own password after logging in. The old password stops working as soon as you click “Confirm password reset”.
- The new password appears only on the “Reset Password” page. The system tries to copy it to the clipboard automatically. Make sure you have saved it before leaving the page.
- Resetting the password ends all login sessions for the account across all devices. Two-step verification and Passkey settings remain in effect, and you still need to complete any required login verification.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- The reset link is valid for 10 minutes and can be used only once. Sending another reset email invalidates the previous link.
- The page displays “Reset email sent. Please check your inbox.” whether or not the address is linked to an account. The site sends an email only if the address is linked to an account.
- “Forgot password?” appears only when the administrator has not disabled password login. The site can send email only after the administrator configures the email service.
</Callout>

## View and End Login Sessions

- Registration and Login – Login Sessions

![Registration and Login – Login Sessions page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/registration-and-login-login-session-management.png)

Each login creates a session for the current device. After logging in, go to “Personal” → “Security & Access” in the sidebar and open “Login Sessions” to see which devices are currently logged in to your account. Click “Revoke” next to another device, “Log out” next to your current device, or “Log out of other sessions”. For instructions and column descriptions, see <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/security#manage-login-sessions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Security &amp; Access</a>.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- If you see a device you do not recognize, revoke its session and change your password.
- Resetting your password ends all login sessions across all devices.
- If too many devices are logged in at the same time, you cannot start another session. See the [FAQ](#frequently-asked-questions) for steps to resolve this.
</Callout>

<Accordion title="The site says ‘Reset email sent’, but you don’t receive the email">
**Message** — “Reset email sent, please check your inbox”

**Note** — To protect account information, the page displays this message whether or not the email address is linked to an account.

**Possible Causes**

- The email address isn’t linked to any account. For example, email verification may have been off when you registered, and you never linked an email address afterward.
- The email went to your spam folder.
- The site’s email service hasn’t been configured, or it failed to send the message.

**What to Do**

1. Make sure you entered the email address linked to your account, then check your spam folder.
2. Wait for the countdown to finish before requesting another email. If you see “Turnstile verification failed, please refresh and try again!”, refresh the page and request the email again.
3. If you can sign in with a third-party account or a Passkey, sign in and use the “Security and Access” page to set a password and link your email address.
4. If the issue persists, contact the administrator.
</Accordion>

| “Invalid reset link, please request a new password reset.” | The link is incomplete. For example, it may have been cut off when you copied it. | Click “here” in the email or copy the full link from the email. |
| The link starts with an address such as `http://localhost:3000` that doesn’t match the current site, and it won’t open | The administrator hasn’t configured the site’s server address. | Replace the address at the start of the link with the current site’s URL and open the resulting link, or contact the administrator. |

<Accordion title="You didn’t save the new password after resetting it">

**What to Do**

1. Return to the “Forgot Password” page and request another reset email.
2. Follow the steps in [Reset a Forgotten Password](#reset-a-forgotten-password) to reset your password again, and copy the new password before leaving the page.
