- In the sidebar, go to “Personal” → “Security & Access”.
- Manage your login password, linked accounts, two-step verification, Passkey, login sessions, and access tokens. You can also choose whether to log IP addresses or delete your account.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- “Access Tokens” start with `nap_` and are used to manage your account from scripts. To call models, use an <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/token" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">API key</a> that starts with `sk-`.

- Third-party account linking and Passkey are available only after the administrator enables the corresponding login methods. See <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/system-settings/settings-auth" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Authentication Configuration</a>.
- If “Security & Access” is missing from the left sidebar:<br/>Go to “Personal Profile”, open the “Personal Sidebar Settings” section, enable “Personal Center” and “Security & Access”, and save your changes.
</Callout>

## Complete security verification

- Security & Access — Security Verification

![Security & Access security verification page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-security-verification.png)

Actions that require verification in the table below open the “Security Verification” dialog with the message “Please verify your identity before performing this sensitive action.” When you delete an account, the dialog is titled “Delete Account”. The screenshot shows three versions of the dialog for different account states.

**Steps**

1. If multiple verification methods are available, select one at the top of the “Security Verification” dialog.
2. Enter the verification code or password for the selected method, or follow your device’s prompts to verify with Passkey.
3. Click “Verify”. If you selected “Linked Account”, click “Continue with …” and complete authorization in the pop-up window.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- After verification succeeds, complete the action within 1 minute. Each verification is valid for a single action. If you change the details of the action, such as the permissions or email address, verify your identity again.
- If your account has two-step verification enabled or a Passkey registered, verification is limited to those two methods.
- If your account has a Passkey registered, “Passkey” is selected by default.
</Callout>

**Verification methods**

| Verification method | When available | What to enter or do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authenticator Code | Two-step verification is enabled on the account. | In “Authenticator Code or Backup Code”, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator or an unused backup code. When regenerating backup codes, enter the 6-digit code. After 5 consecutive incorrect attempts, verification is locked for 5 minutes. |
| Passkey | A Passkey is registered to the account, and the administrator has enabled Passkey. | Follow your browser or operating system prompts to confirm with fingerprint recognition, facial recognition, a PIN, or a security key. If the site has multiple Passkey domains, click “Use a passkey from another website” to select a domain. |
| Password | The account has a password and has neither two-step verification enabled nor a Passkey registered. | Enter your current login password. When changing your password, the value entered in “Current Password” verifies your identity directly, so the “Security Verification” dialog stays closed. |

## Change or set your password

- Security & Access — Change Password

![Security & Access change password page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-change-password.png)

**Steps**

1. Under “Login & Authentication”, click “Change Password”. If your account has no password, click “Set Password”.
2. In the dialog, fill in “Current Password”, “New Password”, and “Confirm New Password”. “Current Password” appears only when you change an existing password.
3. Click “Change Password” or “Set Password” at the bottom of the dialog.
4. Complete verification if the “Security Verification” dialog opens.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- When you change your password, the “Current Password” field verifies your identity directly if you have neither two-step verification enabled nor a Passkey registered. If either is set up, the “Security Verification” dialog opens and requires one of those methods. When you set a password, complete verification in the “Security Verification” dialog.
- After a successful change, all sessions on other devices end and the current device stays signed in. The system sends a notification to the email address on your account.
- If the administrator disables password login, “Password” is unavailable in the “Security Verification” dialog.
</Callout>

## Link your email address and third-party accounts

- Security & Access — Link Email

![Security & Access link email page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-account-binding.png)

“Email” always appears under “Linked Accounts”. GitHub, Discord, OIDC, Telegram, LinuxDO, WeChat, and any other login methods added by the administrator appear only after the administrator enables them.

**Link or change your email address**

1. Click “Link” next to “Email”. If an address is already linked, click “Change”.
2. Enter your email address in “Email Address”, then click “Continue”.
3. Complete verification in the “Security Verification” dialog.
4. In “New Email Verification Code”, enter the 6-digit code sent to your new email address. If you are changing your email address and used neither two-step verification nor Passkey to verify your identity, also enter the code sent to your previous address in “Current Email Verification Code”.
5. Click “Confirm Email”.

**Link a third-party account**

1. Click “Link” next to the third-party account.
2. Complete verification in the “Security Verification” dialog.
3. Click “Continue” in the “Continue Linking Account” dialog.
4. Sign in to the third-party account and authorize the connection in the pop-up window.

**Link WeChat**

1. Click “Link” next to “WeChat”.
2. Scan the QR code in the “Link WeChat” dialog and follow the prompts to get a verification code.
3. Enter the code in “Verification Code” and click “Link”.
4. Complete verification in the “Security Verification” dialog.

**Unlink a third-party account**

1. Click “Unlink” next to the linked account.
2. Confirm in the “Confirm Unlinking” dialog.
3. Complete verification in the “Security Verification” dialog.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Email verification codes are valid for 10 minutes. After 60 seconds, you can click “Resend Code”. After 5 consecutive incorrect attempts, click “Start Over”. Once linking is complete, the system sends notifications to both the new and previous email addresses.
- Once GitHub, Discord, OIDC, Telegram, LinuxDO, or WeChat is linked, the button shows “Linked” and is disabled. Contact the administrator to unlink one of these accounts. The page shows “Unlink” only for other login methods added by the administrator.
- Your account must have at least one login method. If you try to remove the last one, the dialog shows “Add another login method before unlinking this account.”
- Each third-party account can be linked to only one account.
</Callout>

## Enable two-step verification

- Security & Access — Two-Step Verification Disabled

![Security & Access page with two-step verification disabled](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-2fa-not-enabled.png)

- Security & Access — Set Up Two-Factor Authentication

![Security & Access two-factor authentication setup page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-enable-2fa.png)

**Steps**

1. Under “Two-Step Verification”, click “Enable”.
2. Complete verification in the “Security Verification” dialog.
3. In step 1, “Scan QR Code”, use an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator to scan the QR code. You can also click the copy button next to the key and add it to the app manually. Click “Next”.
4. In step 2, “Save Backup Codes”, click “Copy All Codes”, save the 4 backup codes in a secure place, and click “Next”.
5. In step 3, “Verify Setup”, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator in “Verification Code”, then click “Enable 2FA”.

<Callout type="warn" title="Save your backup codes">

- Each backup code can be used once. If your authenticator is unavailable, you can enter a backup code in place of a verification code when signing in or in the “Security Verification” dialog.
- You can view these backup codes only while the dialog is open. If you lose them, regenerate them.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- After completing security verification, finish all three steps within 5 minutes. If the setup times out, the dialog shows “Setup has expired. Scan the new QR code and save the new backup codes.” The system generates a new QR code and new backup codes, and the old ones become invalid.
- The verification code changes every 30 seconds. If your phone’s clock is inaccurate, verification may fail.
- After you enable two-step verification, all sessions on other devices end.
</Callout>

## Regenerate backup codes

- Security & Access — Two-Step Verification Enabled

![Security & Access page with two-step verification enabled](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-2fa-enabled.png)

- Security & Access — Regenerate Backup Codes

![Security & Access regenerate backup codes page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-2fa-regenerate-backup-codes.png)

**Steps**

1. Under “Two-Step Verification”, click “Regenerate Backup Codes”.
2. In the confirmation dialog, click “Generate New Codes”.
3. In the “Security Verification” dialog, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator or verify with Passkey.
4. In the “Your New Backup Codes Are Ready” dialog, click “Copy All Codes”, save the codes, and click “Done”.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Regenerating backup codes invalidates all previous codes and creates 4 new ones. You can view them only while the dialog is open.
- Backup codes are unavailable as a verification method for this action.
- After you regenerate the codes, all sessions on other devices end.
</Callout>

## Disable two-step verification

- Security & Access — Disable Two-Factor Authentication

![Security & Access disable two-factor authentication page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-2fa-disable-2fa.png)

**Steps**

1. Under “Two-Step Verification”, click “Disable 2FA”.
2. In the “Disable Two-Factor Authentication” dialog, select “I understand that disabling 2FA will remove authenticator verification and its backup codes.”
3. Click “Disable 2FA”.
4. In the “Security Verification” dialog, enter a verification code or backup code, or verify with Passkey.

<Callout type="warn" title="Note">

- Disabling two-step verification deletes the authenticator key and all backup codes. Signing in no longer requires a verification code, which reduces your account’s security.
- After you disable two-step verification, all sessions on other devices end. To re-enable it, scan a new QR code with your authenticator app.
</Callout>

## Enable Passkey

- Security & Access — Passkey Disabled

![Security & Access page with Passkey disabled](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-passkey-not-enabled.png)

- Security & Access — Enable Passkey

![Security & Access enable Passkey page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-enable-passkey.png)

**Steps**

1. Under “Passkey Login”, click “Enable Passkey”.
2. Complete verification in the “Security Verification” dialog.
3. Follow your browser or operating system prompts to confirm with fingerprint recognition, facial recognition, a PIN, or a security key.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Passkey is available only after the administrator enables it. Otherwise, the “Security Verification” dialog shows “Passkey authentication is disabled.” See <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/system-settings/settings-auth#passkey-authentication" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Authentication Configuration</a>.
- Browser and operating system prompts vary by device. The screenshot shows the Windows prompt.
- If your device or browser lacks support, the page shows “This device does not support passkeys” and the “Enable Passkey” button is disabled. If you access the site without HTTPS, registration may fail. Contact the administrator.
- You can register one Passkey per account. If you switch devices, remove the existing Passkey first, then register it again on the new device. A Passkey is tied to the website domain used during registration and may stop working if the site’s address changes.
- After you register a Passkey, all sessions on other devices end.
</Callout>

## Manage login sessions

**End a single session**

1. Under “Login Sessions”, click “Revoke” next to another device or “Log Out” next to the current device.
2. Confirm in the confirmation dialog.

**End all other sessions**

1. Click “Log Out of Other Sessions” in the upper-right corner of “Login Sessions”.
2. In the “Log out of other sessions?” dialog, click “Log Out of Other Sessions”.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Ending a session immediately revokes the device’s access. To regain access on that device, sign in again. If you log out of the current device, you are redirected to the login page.
- Ending a session does not require security verification.
- The list shows only sessions that have yet to expire or be ended manually. Sessions become invalid automatically when they expire.
- If you see a device you don’t recognize, revoke its session and change your password.
</Callout>

## Create and Manage Access Tokens

- Security & Access – Access Tokens

![Security & Access – Access Tokens page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-access-token.png)

- Security & Access – Create Access Token

![Security & Access – Create Access Token page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-security-and-access-access-token-create.png)

Use access tokens in scripts to call dashboard APIs for account management, such as retrieving your profile, balance, and logs. Each token has only the permissions selected when it was created. Access tokens cannot be used to call models.

**Create an access token**

1. Click **Create Access Token** in the upper-right corner of **Access Tokens**.
2. Enter a **Token Name** and select an **Expiration**.
3. Under **Permissions**, select the actions you want to grant, such as **View** for **Profile**.
4. Click **Create Access Token** at the bottom of the dialog.
5. Complete verification in the **Security Verification** dialog.
6. In the **Access Token** dialog, click **Copy Token**. Save the token, then click **Close**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Keep your access tokens secure">

- The token is shown only once, when you create it. You cannot view it again after closing the dialog. If you lose it, revoke it and create a new one.
- Anyone who obtains an access token can act on the account within the token’s permissions. Grant **Edit** for **Account Security** with care. It allows actions such as changing the password, changing two-step verification settings, and deleting the account.

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Tips">

- Include `Authorization: Bearer nap_…` in the request headers.
- Each account can have up to 20 access tokens. Tokens that expired within the past 7 days still count toward the limit. At the limit, **Create Access Token** is disabled.
- To manage access tokens or login sessions, sign in to the web interface. Access tokens do not grant access to these operations.

</Callout>

**Edit an access token**

1. Click ⋯ to the right of the token and select **Edit**.
2. In the **Edit Access Token** dialog, change the **Token Name** or **Permissions**.
3. Click **Save**. If you changed the permissions, complete verification in the **Security Verification** dialog.

**Revoke an access token**

1. Click ⋯ to the right of the token and select **Revoke**.
2. Review the prompt in the confirmation dialog, “Revoke this access token? Scripts that use it will stop working immediately,” then confirm the revocation.
3. Complete verification in the **Security Verification** dialog.

<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">

Revoking a token immediately invalidates and deletes it. You cannot restore it. API calls from scripts that use the token will fail immediately.

</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

Legacy tokens created before the upgrade appear at the top of the list with a **Legacy Token** label and the message “Legacy tokens will be disabled on …. Create new tokens to replace them.” Legacy tokens stop working once disabled. Create new tokens to replace them and revoke the legacy tokens.

</Callout>

## View Access Token Activity

- Security & Access – Access Records

**View access records**

3. Click **Details** in the record’s **Details** column to open the **Log Details** dialog.

<Callout type="info" title="Tips">

The top of the drawer shows “Only audit records collected after this feature was enabled appear here. Earlier records are still available in the general logs.”
- These records also appear under **Access Tokens** in the <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/audit-log" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Audit Log</a>. For the contents of the **Log Details** dialog, see **View Audit Details** in that guide.

</Callout>

## Configure IP Address Logging

**Change the setting**

1. In **Privacy**, turn **Log IP Addresses** on or off.
2. Click **Save Settings**.

<Callout type="info" title="Tips">

- This setting is off by default. Once enabled, new **Consumption** and **Error** entries capture the caller’s IP address and show it under **Log Details** in the <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/log" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Usage Log</a>. Existing entries are unaffected.
- This setting applies only to usage logs. IP addresses are always recorded for login sessions, access tokens, and audit logs.

</Callout>

## Delete Your Account

**Delete the account**

1. Click **Delete Account** under **Account Actions**.
2. Enter your full username in the confirmation dialog. The username is case-sensitive.
3. Click **Delete Account**.
4. Complete verification in the **Security Verification** dialog titled **Delete Account**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">

- Account deletion is permanent. All login sessions and access tokens for the account are invalidated immediately, and you are redirected to the sign-in page.
- You cannot register again with the username of a deleted account. If email verification is enabled on the site, you also cannot use the account’s email address to register again. Registration shows “The username already exists or the account has been deleted.”
- Before deleting your account, make sure you no longer need your balance or subscriptions.
- Root accounts cannot be deleted. If you attempt to delete one, the system shows “You do not have permission to perform this action.”

</Callout>
