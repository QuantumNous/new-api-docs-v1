- In the sidebar, go to **Personal** → **Wallet**.
- View your balance, add funds online or redeem quota, purchase subscription plans, and transfer referral rewards.
- Model requests are charged to your wallet balance or subscription quota according to your **Billing Preference**.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- The site determines the quota unit and how currency amounts are displayed. You can use your wallet balance only on the site. Withdrawals are unavailable.
- Online top-ups, redemption codes, subscription plans, and referral rewards are available only after the administrator configures them. Unconfigured features are hidden or display a notice. See <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/system-settings/settings-billing#payment-gateway" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Billing and Payment Configuration</a>.
- If **Wallet** is missing from the left sidebar, go to the **Personal Left Sidebar Settings** section of **Profile**, enable **Personal Center** and **Wallet Management**, and save your changes.
</Callout>

## Add Funds Online

- Wallet – Confirm Payment

![Wallet payment confirmation page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-wallet-add-funds-payment.png)

**Steps**

1. Under **Add Funds**, select a preset **Amount** or enter a **Custom Amount**.
2. Under **Payment Method**, select the method you want to use.
3. In the **Confirm Payment** dialog, review **Top-up Amount**, **You Pay**, and **Payment Method**, then click **Confirm Payment**.
4. Complete the payment on the payment page that opens.
5. Return to the site, click **Order History** in the upper-right corner of **Add Funds**, and verify that the order status is **Success**.

**Steps for paying with Creem**

1. In the **Creem Payment** section, select the product you want to purchase.
2. In the **Confirm Creem Purchase** dialog, review the product, price, and quota, then click the confirmation button.
3. Complete the payment on the payment page that opens.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- The payment page usually opens in a new tab. If your browser blocks the pop-up, allow pop-ups for this site and try again. Waffo Pancake redirects you in the current tab.
- After payment, the page you return to depends on the payment method. You may be taken to the Wallet or Usage Logs page.
- Your balance increases once the payment provider confirms the payment, which may take some time. The page does not refresh automatically. Refresh it later or check **Order History**.
- Too many attempts in a short period can temporarily limit further attempts. Try again later.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Caution">
If your balance still has not increased after an extended period, do not pay again. Keep the order number, proof of payment, and payment time, and contact the administrator to verify the payment.
</Callout>

## Redemption Codes

Use the **Have a redemption code?** section at the bottom of the **Add Funds** card to redeem codes issued by the administrator. The button is labeled **Redeem Quota**.

**Steps**

1. Paste the code into **Enter your redemption code**.
2. Click **Redeem Quota**.
3. When “Redemption successful! Added: …” appears, check the added quota under **Current Balance**.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Paste the entire code without leading or trailing spaces or line breaks. After you click **Redeem Quota**, redemption may take a few seconds.
- Each redemption code can be redeemed successfully only once. Invalid, used, expired, and disabled codes all display “Redemption failed. Please try again later.” For troubleshooting, see [Frequently Asked Questions](#frequently-asked-questions).
- If the administrator has configured a URL for obtaining codes, “Need a redemption code? Get one here” appears below the input field. The link opens in a new tab.
- Successful redemptions create a **Top-up** entry in the usage logs and are excluded from **Billing History**. See <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/log" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Usage Logs</a>.
</Callout>

</Callout>

## Subscription Plans

- Wallet – Purchase a Subscription Plan

![Wallet subscription purchase page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-wallet-subscription-plans-subscribe-now.png)

The **Subscription Plans** card lists the plans published by the administrator. Purchasing a plan grants subscription quota. **My Subscriptions** above the card shows the subscriptions you have purchased. The card is hidden if the administrator has published no plans and the account has no subscription history.

**Pay with your balance**

1. Click **Subscribe Now** at the bottom of a plan card to open the **Purchase Subscription Plan** dialog.
2. Review the plan name, validity period, plan quota, and **Amount Due**.
3. Verify that **Required** is no higher than **Available**, then click **Pay with Balance**.

**Pay with another payment method**

1. Click **Subscribe Now** at the bottom of a plan card to open the **Purchase Subscription Plan** dialog.
2. Under **Select Payment Method**, click **Stripe**, **Creem**, or **Waffo Pancake**. To pay with EPay, select a payment method from the drop-down list, then click **Pay**.
3. Complete the payment on the payment page that opens.
4. Return to **Wallet**, click the refresh icon to the right of **My Subscriptions**, and verify that the new subscription has an **Active** status.

**View subscriptions**

1. Under **My Subscriptions** above the **Subscription Plans** card, check each subscription's status, remaining days, and quota.
2. Click the refresh icon to the right of **My Subscriptions** to update the list.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Paying with your balance creates the subscription immediately, deducts the quota shown under **Required** from **Current Balance**, and creates a **Top-up** entry in the usage logs.
- With other payment methods, the subscription is created after the payment provider confirms the payment. The order appears in **Billing History**.
- **Pay with Balance** is disabled if your balance is insufficient (“Insufficient Balance”), the plan does not allow balance payments (“This plan cannot be purchased with your balance”), or you have reached the purchase limit (“Purchase limit reached (…)”).
- **Select Payment Method** shows only the payment methods the administrator has configured for the plan. If none are available, the section is hidden and you can pay only with your balance.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Caution">

- Subscriptions do not renew automatically when they expire. Purchase the plan again to continue using it.
- For plans purchased through Stripe, Stripe continues to charge you at each billing interval until the subscription is canceled. These charges do not create or extend a subscription in New API. The subscription expires at the end of the plan's validity period. Contact the administrator to stop future charges.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- If the plan has an **Upgrade Group**, the account switches to that Group as soon as the purchase succeeds.
- When a subscription expires or the administrator invalidates it, the account stays in its current Group if it has another active subscription with an Upgrade Group. Otherwise, the account switches to the plan's Downgrade Group if one is configured. If none is configured, the account returns to its pre-purchase Group only if it is still in the Upgrade Group.
- Status and Group changes may take about one minute after expiration.
</Callout>

## Choose a Billing Preference

- Wallet – Choose a Billing Preference

![Wallet billing preference selection page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-wallet-subscription-plans-select-subscription-billing-strategy.png)

**Steps**

1. Click the billing preference drop-down to the right of **My Subscriptions**.
2. Select **Subscription First**, **Wallet First**, **Subscription Only**, or **Wallet Only**.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Your selection is saved immediately without confirmation. It applies to all subsequent requests regardless of which plan you have.
- When you have no active subscriptions, **Subscription First** and **Subscription Only** are disabled and labeled “(None Active)”. If either is your saved preference, the card explains that you have no active subscriptions and that requests will automatically use the wallet or be rejected, depending on your preference.
- A plan can specify whether to allow charges to the wallet balance after the subscription quota is exhausted. This setting is hidden on the Wallet page.
</Callout>

**Billing options**

<div className="[&_table]:min-w-[36rem] [&_td:first-child]:whitespace-nowrap [&_th]:whitespace-nowrap">
| Option | Billing Rule | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription First | If you have an active subscription, requests use subscription quota first. If that quota is insufficient, the request uses the wallet balance only if every active subscription's plan allows it. Otherwise, the request fails. If you have no active subscriptions, requests use the wallet balance. | Default option. Disabled when there are no active subscriptions. |
| Wallet First | Requests use the wallet balance first. If the balance is insufficient, they use subscription quota. | — |
| Subscription Only | Requests use subscription quota only. A request fails if there is no active subscription or the quota is insufficient. | Disabled when there are no active subscriptions. |
| Wallet Only | Requests use the wallet balance only. A request fails if the balance is insufficient. | — |
</div>

## Transfer Referral Rewards

- Wallet – Transfer Rewards

![Wallet reward transfer page](/assets/guide/feature-guide/personal-wallet-referral-program-transfer-to-balance.png)

The **Referral Program** at the bottom of the page shows your referral link and rewards. Rewards earned by the referrer appear under **Pending** and become usable after they are transferred to the wallet balance.

**Invite someone**

1. Click the copy button to the right of the referral link in **Referral Program**.
2. Send the link to the person you want to invite so they can use it to open the registration page and sign up.

**Transfer rewards**

1. Click **Transfer to Balance** in **Referral Program**.
2. In the **Transfer Rewards** dialog, enter the amount you want to transfer in **Transfer Amount**.
3. Click **Transfer**.

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">

- Referral rewards are issued only if the administrator sets a reward quota. By default, no rewards are issued. See <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/system-settings/settings-billing#quota-settings" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Billing and Payment Configuration</a>.
- After an invitee opens the registration page using the referral link, signing up with a username and password or a third-party account counts as a referral. Accounts created through WeChat login are excluded. Any reward for the invitee is credited directly to their balance.
- **Transfer to Balance** appears only when **Pending** is greater than 0. Each transfer must be at least 1 quota unit, shown in the dialog as “Minimum: …” (for example, $1), and cannot exceed **Available Rewards**. If **Pending** is below the minimum, **Transfer** is disabled until enough rewards accumulate.
- Transferred quota goes directly to **Current Balance**. It can only be used on the site, and withdrawals are unavailable.
</Callout>

**Fields**

<div className="[&_table]:min-w-[36rem] [&_td:first-child]:whitespace-nowrap [&_th]:whitespace-nowrap">

| Pending | Referral rewards that have yet to be transferred to your balance. | Increases when an invitee signs up and decreases when rewards are transferred. | Shares its label with the **Pending** order status, which refers to payment confirmation. |
| Total Earnings | The total referral rewards earned. | Calculated automatically by the system. | — |
| Referrals | The number of people who signed up through your referral link. | Calculated automatically by the system. | — |
| Referral Link | The registration link unique to this account. | Generated by the system and cannot be edited. | A copy button appears to the right. |
</div>

**Transfer settings**

The **Transfer Rewards** dialog contains the following setting.

<div className="[&_table]:min-w-[36rem] [&_td:first-child]:whitespace-nowrap [&_th]:whitespace-nowrap">

## Statuses

### Order Status

The order status appears in the upper-right corner of each order in the **Billing History** dialog.

<div className="[&_table]:min-w-[36rem] [&_td:first-child]:whitespace-nowrap [&_th]:whitespace-nowrap">
| Status | Meaning | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pending | The order has been created and is awaiting payment confirmation from the payment provider. | Yellow. Unpaid orders, incomplete payments, and failed payments also show **Pending**. If you have paid and the order remains **Pending** for an extended period, keep the order number and contact the administrator. |
| Success | The order has been paid and the quota has been credited. | Green. |
| Expired | The payment page has expired, and the order will not be credited. | Red. Currently, only Stripe orders can enter this state. Place a new order if you still need to add funds. |
</div>

### Subscription Status

Each subscription in “My Subscriptions” has one of the following statuses.

<div className="[&_table]:min-w-[36rem] [&_td:first-child]:whitespace-nowrap [&_th]:whitespace-nowrap">

| --- | --- | --- |

<Accordion title="Balance does not increase after payment">
**Explanation**

Your balance increases and the order status changes to “Success” once the payment platform notifies the site that the payment succeeded.

**Possible causes**

- The notification from the payment platform has not arrived or has been delayed.
- The payment was incomplete or failed, and the order still shows “Pending Confirmation”.
- The site's payment configuration is incorrect, so the notification cannot reach the site.

**What to do**

1. Do not pay again.
2. Refresh the page after a short wait, then click “Order History” to check the order status.
3. If the order remains “Pending Confirmation” for an extended period, copy the order number and send it to the administrator along with your proof of payment and the time of payment.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Redeeming a code shows “Redemption failed, please try again later”">
**Exact message**

“Redemption failed, please try again later”

**Possible causes**

- The redemption code does not exist, has already been used, has expired, or has been disabled. The same message appears in all four cases.
- The pasted code contains spaces or line breaks.

**What to do**

1. Copy the full redemption code again. Make sure it has no leading or trailing spaces or line breaks, then try again.
2. If redemption still fails, ask the administrator who issued the code to check its status. Avoid repeated attempts, as too many attempts can temporarily restrict access to redemption.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Redeeming a code shows “Top-up is processing, please try again later”">
**Exact message**

“Top-up is processing, please try again later”

**Possible cause**

The account's previous redemption is still being processed.

**What to do**

1. Wait a few seconds and check whether “Current Balance” has increased.
2. If the balance has not increased, click “Redeem Quota” once more. Avoid clicking it repeatedly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your group does not change after purchasing a subscription or revert after it expires">
**Possible causes**

- The plan has no “Upgrade Group” configured, or your account was already in that group before the purchase.
- If you used another payment method, the payment platform may still be waiting to confirm the payment, so the subscription has yet to be created.
- After the subscription expires, your account still has another active subscription with an upgrade group. Alternatively, the plan has no downgrade group and your account's group has been changed.
- Processing a subscription expiration takes about one minute.

**What to do**

1. Check the subscription status in “My Subscriptions”.
2. Wait a few minutes, then refresh the page.
3. If the group still differs from what you expect, contact the administrator.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="API requests fail after the subscription quota runs out">
**Explanation**

When your billing preference is set to “Subscription Only” and you have no active subscription, “My Subscriptions” displays “Preference saved as …, but no active subscription. Requests will be rejected.” This message does not appear if the subscription is still active but has insufficient quota.

**Possible causes**

- Your billing preference is set to “Subscription Only”, and the subscription has expired or its quota has run out.
- Your billing preference is set to “Subscription First”, but one of your active subscription plans does not allow requests to use the wallet balance once the subscription quota runs out.

**What to do**

1. Change your billing preference to “Wallet First” or “Wallet Only”.
2. Purchase a new subscription plan if you want to continue using subscription quota.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Stripe continues to charge you after the plan you purchased expires">
**Explanation**

Stripe automatically charges you at each renewal until the subscription is canceled. Renewal charges do not create or extend a subscription in New API.

**What to do**

1. Keep a record of the charges and contact the administrator to stop future charges and confirm how existing charges will be handled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Referral rewards are unavailable or “Transfer” is disabled">
**Explanation**

Use the behavior you see or the exact message to identify the cause.

| Message | Possible cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Pending Confirmation” shows an amount, but API requests report insufficient quota | You must transfer referral rewards to your balance before you can use them. | Click “Transfer to Balance”. |
| “Transfer” is grayed out, with “Minimum: …” displayed below the input field | “Available Rewards” is less than one unit of quota, or the “Transfer Amount” is out of range. | Adjust the “Transfer Amount”. If the reward balance is too low, wait until more rewards accumulate. |
| “Minimum transfer quota is …!” | The transfer amount is below one unit of quota. | Increase the “Transfer Amount”. |
| “Invitation quota is insufficient!” | The transfer amount exceeds the amount shown under “Pending Confirmation”. | Decrease the “Transfer Amount”. |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="No reward is credited after an invited user signs up">
**Possible causes**

- The administrator has not configured a referral reward amount or has yet to confirm the payment compliance terms. By default, the site does not issue referral rewards.
- The invited user did not open the registration page through the referral link, or they created their account by signing in with WeChat.
- The invited user already had an account before signing up.
- The referrer's reward appears under “Pending Confirmation” and has yet to be transferred to their balance.

**What to do**

1. In “Referral Program”, check the “Invitations” count and the amount under “Pending Confirmation”.
2. If the “Invitations” count has not increased, ask the administrator to check the referral reward settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A record is missing from “Billing History”">
**Possible causes**

- Regular users can only view orders created in the last 30 days.
- Subscription purchases made with your balance, code redemptions, and referral reward transfers do not create orders.
- Search matches only order numbers.

**What to do**

1. Clear the search field and browse the results page by page.
2. To find code redemptions and subscription purchases made with your balance, select “Top-up” as the record type in <a href="/en/docs/guide/feature-guide/user/log" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Usage Logs</a>.
3. Refer to “My Subscriptions” for your subscription details.
</Accordion>
