Welcome to the New API Discord community! Here, you can exchange experiences and share ideas with other developers.

## 📜 Community Rules

To maintain a positive community atmosphere, please adhere to the following rules:

<Callout type="warn" title="Important Notice">
  Violation of the following rules may result in removal from the Discord community. Please read and adhere to them carefully.
</Callout>

1.  Community administrators are not obligated to provide any technical support. If you have questions, please submit an [issue](/en/docs/support/feedback-issues)
2.  Discord discussions should focus on the use, deployment, development, and feedback of New API's open-source project. Do not post information related to account trading, purchase guidance, or solicitations.
3.  This community is solely for open-source community communication. Please obtain project information through official repositories, documentation, and community channels.
4.  New API adopts the GNU AGPLv3 open-source license. It can be used for free as long as the license is complied with. For details, please refer to [Project Introduction](/en/docs/guide/wiki/basic-concepts/project-introduction)
5.  When using this project, you should comply with relevant laws and regulations, upstream terms of service, platform rules, and content security requirements.
6.  Community discussions should revolve around legal authorization, compliant deployment, and legitimate engineering issues. Related messages and member moderation will be handled according to community rules.

<Callout title="Friendly Reminder">
  If you have any feature suggestions or bug feedback, it is recommended to submit an issue directly on GitHub, which allows for better tracking and resolution of problems.
</Callout>
